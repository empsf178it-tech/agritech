Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about requesting an AGROVIA demo.

How long does the live demo session take? ✚

Our standard demo takes 25 to 30 minutes. We leave plenty of time for Q&A and can customize the session length based on your schedule and interest areas.

Do I need to install any hardware to see a demo? ✚

No hardware required! We construct a virtual digital twin using satellite imagery and historical microclimate data for your specific region so you can see realistic data immediately.

Can I invite my farm manager or agronomist? ✚

Absolutely! We encourage team participation including farm operators, agronomists, and irrigation managers so everyone can see how the platform fits their daily routines.

Is there any cost or commitment for the demo? ✚

The demo is 100% free with zero commitment. Our goal is to demonstrate how AGROVIA can increase your yield and lower input costs before you make any decisions.
